Assumptions & Exclusions

What this estimate assumes

This planning estimate is built around the production approach outlined below. It keeps the budget flexible while the creative direction and logistics are being confirmed.

  • One full shoot day plus up to one additional half shoot day.
  • Creative development, pre-production, production leadership, lighting/grip and second-camera coverage, editing, color/finishing, and delivery.
  • A $2,000 production expense allowance for locations/permits, art and props, specialty rentals, meals, transportation, and related production needs.
  • Not included: major travel, outside talent, special effects, extensive art builds, or premium locations.
  • Any material change to scope or production expenses requires written approval before spend.

Approval boundary

This is a planning estimate, not a fixed bid. Once we approve the creative direction and production plan, TruMedia Creative will confirm the final scope, budget, and contract terms.
